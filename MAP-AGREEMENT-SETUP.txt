
PLATEAU MAP AGREEMENT SETUP

1) Open your Google Sheet:
   https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1kqDyR-yA8fDWGRLKasRk0zzkjF-emD9kH-nKtL3uDrw/edit?gid=0#gid=0

2) In Google Sheets go to Extensions > Apps Script.

3) Paste in the contents of plateau-map-agreement.gs and save.

4) Deploy > New deployment > Web app.
   - Execute as: Me
   - Who has access: Anyone

5) Copy the web app URL.

6) In /maps/advertising-agreement.html replace:
   YOUR_GOOGLE_APPS_SCRIPT_URL
   with your live web app URL.

7) Optional: change the default internal copy email in the HTML and Apps Script if needed.

What this setup does:
- Captures advertiser agreement details from the website
- Logs every submission to a Google Sheet tab called Map Agreements
- Auto-generates an invoice number if one is not entered
- Emails a copy of the agreement to the advertiser and your internal email address

Important note:
This is a practical typed-signature workflow. If you need deeper legal e-sign compliance, audit trails, or payment collection at signing, use DocuSign, PandaDoc, Jotform Sign, or Stripe checkout in the next phase.
